Privacy notice

This notice describes the default static AdMuse Studio website.

Project enquiries

The enquiry form prepares a project brief in your browser. It does not transmit or store the form on a server. If email or WhatsApp is configured, the form opens a draft in that service; you choose whether to send it. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Local preferences

Your selected display theme may be saved in your browser's local storage. The default site does not include advertising pixels or analytics scripts.

Stock photography

Some decorative stock photographs load from the Pexels image CDN, which may receive technical request information such as your IP address. See Pexels privacy policy.

Hosting

The website hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser information in accordance with its own policies.

Contact

Use the project enquiry section for privacy-related questions.

This notice should be updated to reflect the actual hosting, analytics, enquiry handling and data retention practices before public launch.
