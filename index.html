07 / GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Not sure where to begin? We can help you choose the right setup.

Can I use my existing domain and email?

Yes. We can work with your existing domain registrar, DNS provider and email service, or help you migrate where appropriate.

Can I purchase a domain directly here?

For now, send a domain registration enquiry. We will confirm availability, renewal pricing and registrar options before you pay.

Do you use AI to develop websites?

Yes, where useful. AI helps accelerate design and development, while code review, testing and project decisions remain part of the delivery process.

Do I own my website and domain?

Ownership, access and any third-party licences are agreed in your proposal. We recommend registering your domain in your own name and keeping access to your accounts.

Can you automate WhatsApp or customer enquiries?

Yes, subject to the available official APIs, provider policies and your business requirements. Usage charges and integrations are quoted separately.

Can I send my own footage for editing?

Absolutely. Share your raw footage and creative brief; we can edit reels, Shorts or vlogs remotely. Deliverables and revision limits will be agreed before work begins.

Where are shoots available?

Local shoots are based in Hyderabad. Destination and outstation shoots can be arranged by request, with travel costs quoted separately. Drone work depends on applicable rules and permissions.

Do you guarantee follower growth or ad results?

No. We help with content, targeting, optimisation and reporting, but results depend on many factors. Ad spend is separate from management fees.

Can I join a creator trip without experience?

Yes, you can register interest as a beginner. Each experience will have its own details, costs, availability and participation requirements.

How much time does a project take?

It depends on the scope, content readiness and revisions. We confirm a timeline in the project proposal before development begins.
